Security Tips

Hover before you click

Always hover over a link to reveal the true destination URL before you click.

⚠️ Look for misspelled domains, excessive subdomains, or links that don't match the sender's company.

Enable MFA

Multi-factor authentication blocks most account takeover attempts.

🔐 Use an authenticator app over SMS codes whenever possible.

Report suspicions

Report any unusual, urgent, or unexpected email immediately.

🚨 Use the report button in your mailbox or contact the security team right away.
